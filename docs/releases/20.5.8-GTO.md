## 中文

**仅支持 GregTech-Odyssey 0.6.0 dev30 以上版本。**

Neo ECO AE Extension **20.5.8-GTO**，适用于 **Minecraft 1.20.1 / Forge**，游戏运行需要 **Java 21**。

- 添加实时合成图，显示执行进度、完成标记和精确材料数量；支持浅色与深色主题，改善循环节点布局、连线和箭头。
- 合成确认界面支持一键将缺失材料加入 EMI 收藏。
- 添加大型集成工作站智能冷却控制器、超低温铝合金机械外壳和相变型空冷散热器。控制器支持 0–99% 目标温度、标记冷却液和 ME 自动补液。
- 智能温控可在待机时升降温并恒温，冷却液缓存为 16,384,000 mB；每 tick 消耗 100,000 AE 和至少 300 mB 冷却液，热负载增加时提高耗液量。
- 优化工作站批次执行与同任务合批，将新接收配方的加工时长调整为 1 tick，按实际付费加工 tick 结算产热，并修复能量退款计量。
- 为工作站配方添加产物索引和数量匹配缓存，减少重复遍历与分配；配方重载时同步更新缓存。
- 完善工作站指南，支持直接显示物品、流体、精确数量、能耗和时长的配方卡片。
- 修复 GTO 工作站设置按钮图标、界面长整数同步及二合一终端样板上传。
- 调整赋能超导锭配方使用硅粉、铌钛锭和氦气，补充铝锭研磨配方。

## English

**Only GregTech-Odyssey 0.6.0 dev30 and newer are supported.**

Neo ECO AE Extension **20.5.8-GTO** for **Minecraft 1.20.1 / Forge**. The game requires **Java 21**.

- Add live crafting graphs with execution progress, completion markers and exact material amounts. Support light and dark themes and improve cycle layouts, routes and arrowheads.
- Add a button to bookmark missing crafting materials in EMI.
- Add the large workstation smart cooling controller, cryogenic aluminum alloy casing and phase-change air cooler. Configure a target from 0–99%, mark coolant and refill it automatically from ME.
- Smart regulation heats, cools and holds the target even while idle. Its buffer holds 16,384,000 mB; operation costs 100,000 AE/t and at least 300 mB/t of coolant, with more coolant used under higher heat loads.
- Improve workstation batching within the same job, process newly accepted recipes in 1 tick, settle heat for paid processing ticks and correct energy refunds.
- Index workstation recipes by output and cache quantity contracts, refreshing the caches with recipe reloads.
- Expand the workstation guide with recipe cards showing items, fluids, exact amounts, energy and duration.
- Fix GTO workstation setting icons, long integer UI synchronization and pattern upload in the two-in-one terminal.
- Use silicon dust, niobium titanium ingots and helium in the energized superconductive ingot recipe and add aluminum ingot grinding.
